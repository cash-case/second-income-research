This uses permitted non-Amazon, non-Trends sources only. It doesn't estimate market size or sales, and nothing here is demand evidence. The labels mark what kind of source each item comes from: articles and templates [V that the source says it], or my judgment [A].

Why people keep records [S, guidance articles, not buyer voices]:
- Resale ("worth a premium", per Capital One quoting AutoPacific).
- Mechanics checking history.
- Avoiding repeat diagnostics.
- Warranty and recall proof (motorist.org).

Recurring record-keeping problems the guides name [S]:
- Paper "lost in the glove box".
- Relying on memory.
- Invoices missing mileage.
- "a stack of disconnected papers" with no pattern.
- Systems people stop keeping up.

[A] The problems the bound format can address: one place for everything, mileage written at the time of service, next-due visible at a glance, and a vehicle info page. A **receipt pocket or envelope can't be done in KDP paperback**, so it isn't claimable.

## Gap vs current drafts [A]
The vehicle draft has 4 of the 12 catalogued features. The expense draft has 5 of 8 fields. A minimum-parity revision:
- **Vehicle:** add a vehicle info page, a next-due column, and a shop column, plus a back-section service-interval grid.
- **Expense:** add vendor and payment-method columns, plus a page total.

This still differentiates only by being bound, versus free printables [T].
