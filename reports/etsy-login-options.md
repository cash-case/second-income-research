**This is Cash's decision.** It's his delegation policy (list (a)). Nothing here is set up, and no shop exists.
Labels: [V] verified at source, [S] secondhand, [A] judgment or assumption, [T] test needed.

**Facts every option shares:**
- Opening the shop needs Cash's identity and payout verification: legal name, DOB, address, SSN or tax ID, bank account, possibly an ID photo [V help.etsy.com]. This step is his alone, one-time, plus any re-verification Etsy asks for.
- Buyers wait 48 hours before they can open a case. The seller is expected to respond regularly [V etsy.com cases policy]. The seller's own deadline isn't verified.
- How often buyers message or open cases is **unknown until there are sales**. No option can be scored "zero labor".
- **Unverified for all options:** whether Etsy's terms allow someone other than the account holder to operate the shop, and whether AI-drafted replies sent by a person comply with the Oct 22 human-involvement policy [T: read Etsy's Terms of Use and Seller Policy at source].
