# ESV and The Message: rights holders' own published terms (reference, 2026-11-06)

This is a reference for comparing against the official rights-holder check the research owner is running. It doesn't replace that check. **Nothing here clears any text.** Text-cleared marks stay on hold, and full verses aren't rendered into designs until written permission exists.

## ESV (Crossway)
Owning pages: https://www.crossway.org/permissions/ (also mirrored at https://www.esv.org/churches/permissions/) and the request form at https://www.crossway.org/permissions/artwork/

| Point | What Crossway's page says [V] |
|---|---|
| Gratis allowance | Up to and including 500 verses without written permission, as long as they're not more than half of any one book and not 25% or more of the work. It doesn't apply to commentaries or reference works. For artwork and music, the limits apply **per single product**. |
| **Art and stationery** | **Written permission or a formal licence is required** for "greeting cards, paintings, photography prints, digital artwork, calendars, and planners". [A] Wall-art prints, POD products and planner-style journals fall under this line. **The gratis allowance doesn't cover them.** |
| Merchandise, apparel, journals | Not named. [A] Treat as needing permission until Crossway says otherwise. |
| Trademark | "ESV" and "English Standard Version" are registered trademarks of Crossway, and using either needs Crossway's permission. |
| Notice | A long-form notice for saleable media; the full text is on the page. The "(ESV)" initials-only form applies only to **non-saleable** media. |
| What the artwork form asks for | Product line type, materials and media, number of unique products and copies of each, approximate size, verses per product, % of text from the ESV, retail price, whether it will be sold, formats, **all distribution channels**, whether the ESV text will be edited, and **whether generative AI is used**. |
| Fees and turnaround | Not stated. |
| ESV API | Free for non-commercial use only, so it isn't a source of text for products. |

## The Message (NavPress, represented by Tyndale)
Owning page: https://www.tyndale.com/permissions (messagebible.com/faq has no permission terms [V])

| Point | What Tyndale's page says [V] |
|---|---|
| Gratis allowance (print and eBook) | Up to 500 verses, no more than 25% of the work, and no complete book. |
| **Art and merchandise** | **Written permission is required** for quotes on "T-shirts, book covers, posters, bumper stickers, banners, greeting cards, billboards, or other artwork". You request it through the **Permissions Questionnaire**, giving product types, the retail price of each, **expected units over five years**, and the % of each product that's MSG. |
| Journals | Not named specifically. [A] A journal that prints daily MSG verses could fall under the print allowance, as a work with no more than 25% MSG text, but whether a journal's text share counts that way is **[T] for Tyndale to answer.** |
| Online and audio | Questionnaire required; no standard allowance. |
| Fees | Products for sale beyond fair-use limits "may require a fee", based on amount, distribution, and commercial vs non-commercial use. |
| Accuracy | Quote exactly, including punctuation, capitalization and italics. Notify Tyndale in writing before any style deviation. |
| Scope | Permission covers only the specific use requested and can't be transferred. |
| Notice | Exact credit lines are on the page (the exclusive-use form names Eugene H. Peterson and NavPress, "Represented by Tyndale House Publishers"). |

## What this means for the lineup [A]
- **Wall art and POD (both translations):** written permission is needed before any design carries the verse text. Both forms ask for channels, prices and volumes, so the channel report (`scripture-wall-art-channels.md`) and the product list feed straight into the requests.
- **Requests need decided facts:** product types, sizes, prices, channels, five-year unit estimates (MSG), and AI use (ESV). Those are Cash's answers or the team's agreed numbers, not guesses. Any unit estimate submitted should be labeled as an estimate.
- **Journals:** the MSG print allowance may apply, ESV's "planners" line may not, and both are [T] with the rights holders.
- **Until then**, drafts use verse references and placeholders only, as Instinct stated.
