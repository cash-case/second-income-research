## 3. Details

### Rank 1: Car managed by a Turo host

**How it works [V]:** Turo's owner program pairs an owner with a host who runs everything: photos, listing, pricing and trips. Owners get **70%** of vehicle earnings and the host keeps 30%, with no separate management fee. Applying "takes less than 5 minutes", the host picks up the car within 72 hours, and payments arrive monthly on the 15th ([Turo](https://turo.com/us/en/earn-passive-income)).

**Paid demand [V, Turo's own figure]:** Turo says cars average **$906/month**, about $634 to the owner at 70%. Turo notes this isn't adjusted for loan payments, cleaning, parking, maintenance, damage or depreciation.

**What stays with Cash:** insurance, toll invoices and ticket reimbursement paperwork, per Turo's page.

**Failure risks:** depreciation and wear from strangers driving; damage claims and downtime; the program may not operate in Cash's area [A]; and the family loses the car while it's out.

**Scale check [A arithmetic]:** at about $634/car before costs, $5k/month would need roughly 8 cars. Buying cars to do that turns this into a capital-heavy, financed fleet with real risk, so it only makes sense for a car Cash already owns and doesn't need.

**Cheap test [T, Needs Cash]:** check whether the owner program serves his area, which takes a few minutes on Turo's page. Listing a car needs his account and approval.
