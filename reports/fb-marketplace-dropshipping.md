# Facebook Marketplace dropshipping: Claude's input for the synthesis (research only, 2026-11-07)

**Source limits:** facebook.com help pages are robots-disallowed for me, and Meta's Transparency Center policy pages render no text to my fetcher (JavaScript only). **Nothing below is verified at a Meta owning source.** Everything is [S] secondary unless marked, and several sources sell dropshipping tools or memberships, which is a commercial bias. Instinct is reading the official pages in parallel; those readings should override anything here.

## 1. Is it allowed (US / Colorado)?
- [S] SaleHoo (updated Aug 19 2026; sells supplier memberships): "No Meta policy prohibits it, and none endorses it either". Meta's Commerce Policies "are silent on whether the item is in your spare room or in a warehouse".
- [S] AutoDS (Sep 2026; sells dropshipping software): "every listing needs to represent an actual physical product for sale". The cited rules are to mark orders shipped with valid tracking **within 3 business days**, have the item reach the carrier within **7 calendar days** or face auto-cancel, and that unmarked orders auto-cancel after 30 days.
- [S] HackerNoon (Jul 17 2026; sponsored opinion piece) doesn't state any Meta policy on dropshipping either way.
- **[T] Status:** no secondary source quotes Meta text that allows *or* prohibits dropshipping. Instinct's owning-source read decides this. Enforcement in practice: see section 4.

## 2. Supplier economics
- **Selling fee on shipped orders conflicts across sources.** A Meta notice quoted by Value Added Resource (no Meta link given) says: "will increase to 10%, or a minimum of $0.80 per listing", effective **Apr 15 2024**. SaleHoo says the 10% applies to the full buyer total including shipping and tax. Voolist (Apr 2026, no Meta citation) still says **5%**. **[T] The current rate needs Meta's page.** Local pickup is reported as free [S], but that doesn't apply to dropshipping, which needs shipping.
- **Shipping labels:** Meta ended prepaid labels for new listings on **Feb 24 2025** and partially reinstated them for "select" sellers in Sep 2025 [S, Value Added Resource, which links a facebook.com help page]. Dropshippers use the supplier's tracking.
- **Payout timing:** SaleHoo says about 5 days after delivery is confirmed, with a fallback around 15–20 days after shipment if the buyer doesn't confirm, plus 1–5 days of bank settlement. Voolist says 1–5 business days after confirmation. **Sources disagree [S].** [A] **The supplier is paid upfront, so each sale is cash-out first, and the money comes back days or weeks later.** That's a float the "no upfront inventory" constraint doesn't remove.
- **Returns and disputes:** SaleHoo reports Purchase Protection claims open **up to about 45 days after delivery**, about 10 days for the seller to respond, and a lost chargeback costing **order value + $20** [S]. [A] Supplier return policies often don't match the buyer's window, so the seller absorbs the gap.
- **Margin:** no credible per-sale figure found. [A] On an illustrative $40 shipped sale at a 10% fee on the total, the fee is about $4 *before* supplier cost, supplier shipping, returns and chargebacks. A real margin needs a named supplier's prices, which is [T].

## 3. Daily workload [A on S inputs]
- Per order: **forward the order to the supplier, then post the tracking within 3 business days**, every day orders come in (automation tools exist, but they're paid and connect to his account).
- **Buyer messages arrive in Messenger on his personal Facebook profile.** Marketplace buyers expect fast replies (ask whether it's still available, haggle, ask where the order is).
- Disputes and claims: respond within about 10 days, with evidence from a supplier he doesn't control.
- Listing upkeep: prices and stock must track the supplier, or he sells items that are out of stock.
- **Hands-off fit: poor.** Every touchpoint is recurring, it's on Cash's personal Facebook identity, and a lapse in the 3-day rule auto-cancels orders and hurts his account metrics.

## 4. Failure modes, last 6–9 months
- [S] SaleHoo: accounts are rated Good, Needs Improvement or Requires Action; penalties escalate from reduced visibility to full selling restrictions; there are seven seller metrics (late handling, late delivery, chargebacks, negative feedback…); Meta hasn't published thresholds for personal Marketplace profiles.
- [S] AutoDS: falling short "can temporarily cost you access to shipping altogether".
- [S] HackerNoon: overseas suppliers take 2–3 weeks against 2–3 days domestic (the figure comes from Doba, a supplier). Slow shippers churn out in about 3 months (uncited).

## Reading [A], preliminary
On Cash's three constraints:
- **Hands-off:** fails. Daily order forwarding, Messenger replies and disputes, all on his personal profile.
- **No upfront inventory:** technically met, but every sale needs upfront supplier payment, and payout lags days to weeks.
- **Concrete viable income:** unproven. There are no verified fees, no supplier margins and no demand data.

I'd rank it below the Etsy text-art track, which at least has a defined product and owning-source fees. The synthesis should wait for Instinct's Meta owning-source read on policy and fees.
