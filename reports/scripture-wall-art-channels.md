## Fee arithmetic on one illustrative $25 order, no shipping or tax [A]
This is not a price recommendation or a projection.
- **Etsy:** 0.20 + 1.63 + 1.00 = **$2.83 (11.3%)**. If the sale came through Offsite Ads, add $3.75, for **$6.58 (26.3%)**.
- **Shopify Basic:** **$1.03** per order, plus $29–39/month whether or not anything sells.
- **Break-even:** Shopify saves about $1.80 per $25 order versus Etsy, so the $29/month plan needs about **16 orders/month** before it's cheaper, and that's *before* any traffic cost. Shopify brings no buyers by itself.

## Reading [A]
- **Etsy is the only route with verified built-in buyers.** It also has the highest per-sale cost, rising to 26% with Offsite Ads, and the heaviest message and case duties.
- **Shopify** costs less per sale but carries a fixed monthly cost and zero native traffic. On the hands-off standard it **adds** recurring work (traffic, disputes), so it isn't a fit as the first channel. It could make sense later, if a proven Etsy product justifies it.
- **Pinterest** costs nothing and fits as a **traffic source** feeding Etsy (or a future Shopify store). It isn't a storefront. Its value for scripture art is [T].
