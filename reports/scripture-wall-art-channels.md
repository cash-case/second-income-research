## Fee arithmetic on one illustrative $25 order, no shipping or tax [A]
**This is fee arithmetic only. It isn't profit, margin or break-even.** POD fulfillment cost, shipping, and the cost of getting buyers aren't included. Any recommendation built on it stays preliminary.
- **Etsy:** 0.20 + 1.63 + 1.00 = **$2.83 (11.3%)**. If the sale came through Offsite Ads, add $3.75, for **$6.58 (26.3%)**.
- **eBay, no Store:** 3.40 + 0.40 = **$3.80 (15.2%)**. eBay's page names no separate payment processing fee, so I've assumed none, but that's [T]. Promoted Listings would cost extra at a rate not stated on that page.
- **Shopify Basic:** **$1.03** in card fees, plus the $29–39/month plan whether or not anything sells.
- The fee gap between Etsy and Shopify is about $1.80 per $25 order. Fixed-plan coverage at about 16 orders/month is fee arithmetic only, not a break-even.

## Reading [A], preliminary
- **Etsy and eBay** are the marketplace routes with their own buyer search. Etsy's fees are lower without Offsite Ads (11.3% vs eBay's 15.2% on the illustration) and higher with them (26.3%). eBay adds a fee penalty tied to performance standards.
- **Shopify** costs less per sale but carries a fixed monthly cost. A new store's traffic depends on channels and apps whose effect is [T]. On the hands-off standard it **adds** recurring work (traffic, disputes), so it doesn't fit as the first channel.
- **Pinterest** costs nothing and fits as a **traffic source** feeding a store. It isn't a storefront. Its value for scripture art is [T].
- **Facebook/Instagram** is blocked (robots) and remains [T].
- None of this is demand evidence. The recommendation stays preliminary until POD costs, margins and buyer-acquisition costs are in.
