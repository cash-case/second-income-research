# Small landlord year-end ledger (Schedule E categories): draft

**Status:** draft for review. Not for sale. Nothing is listed or launched.

## Angle
The landlord niche has strong purchase evidence: 26 item reviews on a listing from a shop with 7.1k sales. It is also crowded and sells at about $5. This draft competes on **year-end tax prep**. Expenses use the IRS Schedule E expense-line categories, and the summary gives one column per property, ready to hand to a tax preparer.

## Sheets
- **Instructions:** blank for the seller to write. It includes a "not tax advice" note.
- **Settings:** the tax year.
- **Properties:** up to 20.
- **Income:** 1,000 rows of rent, late fees, pet fees and other income.
- **Expenses:** 2,000 rows, with property and category dropdowns.
- **Year-End Summary:** income, each Schedule E category, total expenses and net, per property (up to 10 columns) plus an all-properties total, filtered to the tax year.

## Verified
All formulas were recalculated in LibreOffice. With the examples, Unit A shows $1,450 income, $225 repairs and $1,225 net. The duplex shows $2,200 income, $960 insurance and $1,240 net. The all-properties total is $2,465.

## Risks
- **Tax content.** The category names must be checked against the current IRS Schedule E. **Needs an expert** (Instinct to verify against irs.gov) before any listing. Not tax advice.
- **Crowded niche.** Price pressure is real.
