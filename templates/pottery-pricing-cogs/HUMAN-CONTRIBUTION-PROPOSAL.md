# Pottery pricing template: proposed human-contribution design (PROPOSAL, not a decision)

**Why this exists:** Etsy's own texts (read at source Nov 6; see reports/etsy-ai-policy-scope.md on PR #2) say:
- Digital items must be "original content created by the seller", and AI creations must be "based on a seller's original prompts" [V Creativity Standards].
- "The information buyers rely on to create or use your item must be your own work, not generated by AI", and you must "show an actual result" [V Oct 22 2026 update].
- AI use must be disclosed [V].

**Interpretation [A, not legal advice]:** the current draft doesn't meet these yet, because the prompts and structure came from the team, not from Cash.

## Proposed split

| Part | Who | Why | Estimated effort [A, not measured] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design brief: what costs he tracks, how he prices, what he wants the sheet to do | **Cash, in his words** (spoken or typed, any length) | Makes the item "based on the seller's original prompts" | One sitting |
| Building the workbook to his brief | Team (AI) | Allowed as AI-assisted creation with disclosure | — |
| Instructions sheet text | **Cash, in his words** (the team can ask questions but doesn't write it) | "Information buyers rely on ... must be your own work" | One sitting |
| A real example: one of his actual pieces, with his actual clay, glaze, kiln and labor numbers | **Cash supplies the numbers**; the team enters them | "Show an actual result", not a simulated one | Short |
| Listing photos / screenshots of the real example | Team | Real content, not simulated | — |
| Listing description, with an accurate AI disclosure of who did what | Team drafts; **Cash approves** | Accuracy of the disclosure | Short review |

**What this is not:** a recurring job. It's one-time per product. It only works where Cash has real experience and data, which means pottery. The chicken, landlord and glaze-recipe drafts would need a seller with real experience in those areas, or they stay unlisted.

**Still unresolved:** whether formulas count as a "functional guide"; who answers buyer messages and cases (Etsy shared access is for *human* team members; refunds stay with the owner [V]); whether this one-time load fits Cash's hands-off brief. That last one is his call. This goes to him only after Instinct judges the proposal is grounded.

**Important:** private financial records never go to an external service or into this public repo. The real example would use piece-level studio costs that Cash chooses to publish in a listing, nothing else.
