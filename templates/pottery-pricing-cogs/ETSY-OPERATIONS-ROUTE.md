# Etsy pottery template: operations route through permitted access (PROPOSAL, Nov 6)

Grounded in Etsy's own pages, read by Claude on Nov 6. [V] = owning page, [A] = our judgment.

## What Etsy's shared access permits [V]
Sources: help.etsy.com 43600112070807 ("Shared Access for Etsy Shop Owners") and Seller Handbook 1502631241595.
- Up to 5 team members, each a **person aged 18+** on **their own Etsy account**, with 2FA turned on, and no shop of their own on that email.
- Members **can:** view and reply to buyer messages; create, edit and publish listings; manage orders.
- Members **can't:** cancel or refund orders; see Finances; change settings; invite others.
- "The shop owner is responsible for activity in their shop, including actions taken by team members." The owner can download members' message, order and listing activity as CSV files.
- Cases aren't mentioned, so whether members can act on them is unverified.

## How buyer cases work [V help.etsy.com 360016126873]
- Cases are handled in Shop Manager > Help > Cases. **Etsy reviews the case and makes the determination.** "You won't need to issue the refund yourself." Etsy may resolve a case on the seller's behalf and recoup the funds.
- No seller response deadline is stated. If Etsy asks for information, "you must respond."
- Non-delivery cases rest on proof of delivery [V]. For an instant digital download, Etsy delivers the file itself [A, the download mechanics weren't read].

## Proposed coverage [A]
| Function | Who | Cash's load |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer messages | A **human** team member on shared access sends replies; AI drafts them | None, if a member exists |
| Listing edits | The team member, with AI-drafted content | None |
| Refunds and cancellations | **Owner only** [V]. For a digital file, the expected volume is low [A, unmeasured] | Occasional, frequency unknown |
| Cases | Etsy determines the outcome [V]. Whether the team member can comment is unverified [T]. Otherwise the owner | Occasional, frequency unknown |
| Finances and payout | Owner only [V] | Monthly glance at most [A] |

## What needs Cash in person vs what can be delegated [V/A]
- **In person:** identity and payout verification (legal name, DOB, address, tax ID, bank, possibly an ID photo) [V help.etsy.com verification]; being the accountable owner [V]; refunds and cancellations [V].
- **Can be delegated:** messages, listings and orders, to a **human** member [V]. There's no stated route for an **AI agent** acting as a member. ToS 6.C says automated agents' actions "are considered your actions", which is a liability statement, not a permission [V].

## Documented dead end
**Fully AI-operated with zero human is not available through the permitted route.** The member feature requires an 18+ person with their own account and 2FA [V]. The best compliant shape is AI drafting plus one human member, with Cash keeping refunds, finances and accountability.

## Open

- Whether members can act on cases [T].
